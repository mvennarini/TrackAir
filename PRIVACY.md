# TrackAir – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

TrackAir turns your iPhone or iPad into a trackpad for your Mac.

**What TrackAir collects: nothing.** TrackAir has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, no crash reporting and no servers. It does not collect, store or
transmit any personal data.

**What travels over the network.** Touch movements, clicks and gestures are
sent from your iPhone or iPad to your Mac over your local Wi-Fi network only.
This traffic is encrypted end to end with ChaCha20-Poly1305 using keys created
during a one-time pairing protected by a PIN shown on your Mac. Nothing is sent
to the internet.

**What is stored on your devices.** After pairing, each device keeps the name
and the encryption keys of the devices it is paired with, in its own private
storage. You can delete them at any time ("Forget" in the app settings, or
"Paired devices" in the Mac menu). TrackAir also stores your gesture preferences.

**Permissions.** On iOS, TrackAir asks for Local Network access to find your Mac
with Bonjour. On macOS, TrackAir needs the Accessibility permission to move the
pointer and send clicks; this is the only way an app can control the pointer.

**Contact.** Open an issue on the GitHub repository (github.com/mvennarini/TrackAir).
